About

Candlelit.

Candlelit Trading builds research and trading software for digital-asset markets. Its first product is Wick.

What we are

Candlelit is a small, independent software project. It builds tools for understanding cryptocurrency markets and for practising the discipline that trading them well requires: research before conviction, structure before size, and a decision that stays with the person making it.

What we are not

Candlelit is not a brokerage, an exchange, a fund, or a proprietary trading firm. It does not manage money, hold client assets, provide funded accounts, or give financial advice. Wick executes simulated trades only.

Why Wick exists

Wick began as a market-data terminal and became a guided desk for practising prop-firm-style challenges. Along the way it turned into something its builder had not planned: a way to learn trading by operating a real workflow with real market data, where every concept is explained at the moment it is needed. That is the thing we are most interested in continuing to build.
