Privacy

Privacy, briefly.

Effective 9 September 2026. We collect as close to nothing as a working application allows.

The website (candlelit.us)

The website is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and contains no tracking pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving the font files. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, user agent, requested page) for security and operation.

The application (app.candlelit.us)

Wick requires a password. After sign-in it sets one session cookie, used only to keep you signed in; it contains no personal information beyond a signed session identifier and expires after thirty days. Wick stores what you do in it: simulated accounts, trades, notes, and research results. It does not collect your name, email, or payment details, and it runs no analytics. Requests for AI research send market numbers about a coin, never anything about you, to OpenAI's API under OpenAI's terms. The application is hosted on Railway.

Email

If you write to tradingcandlelight@gmail.com, we keep the correspondence for as long as it is useful to reply to you.

Your choices

Clear the session cookie to sign out. Ask us to delete your simulated data at any time by email.
