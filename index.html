04 · Learn by Operating

Trading makes more sense when the concept is attached to a decision.

Wick is designed for learning through use. ATR, funding, order flow, reward-to-risk, invalidation, position sizing, and drawdown are surfaced at the moment they matter, in plain English, on the screen where the decision is made.

The terminal handles the arithmetic and organizes the evidence. You make the decision.

ATROne typical day's range. Wick measures every move, stop and target in it.
FundingWhat perpetual longs pay shorts, or the reverse. Crowded when extreme.
Taker flowThe share of volume that was aggressive buying. Who is in a hurry.
InvalidationThe price at which the idea is wrong. Set before size, never after.
Reward-to-riskWhat the target pays for each unit the stop can lose.
Position sizingSize so that a stop-out costs a fixed slice of equity.
DrawdownHow far equity sits below its peak. The rule that ends challenges.
